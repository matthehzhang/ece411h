# ECE 411H (UT Austin, Fall 2026): transcripts and notes for the Semester Exam 2 unit

Text-only material on semiconductors, transfer functions, Bode plots, and passive and active filters, plus the lecture notes of the Exam 1 unit.

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `Transcripts/` | Transcriptions of image-only course PDFs: past Exam 2s (F23, F24) and finals (F23 to F25) with short answers, the F26 Exam 1 histogram and short answers, PS5 to PS8 statements, PS5 to PS7 official solutions, the instructor's Ch 9, 12 and 13 problem-solving videos, recitations 4 and 5, refreshers. `INDEX.md` lists them. |
| `Recordings/<date>/` | Machine transcripts of the lecture recordings, a quality report per lecture (`QUALITY.md`, read it first), and board pages aligned with the speech and described in words (`frames/*.md`; images not included). |
| `Problem Solving Videos/` | Captions of the instructor's Ch 4 to 8 videos. |
| `ece411/` | Distilled notes: one note per lecture (`Lectures/`), past-exam analysis (`Exam Intel.md`), what the instructor emphasises and how he grades (`Instructor Emphasis*.md`), PS6 and PS7 worked solutions with variant drills, vetted external videos (`Explainers.md`), logistics (`Course Map.md`), Ch 9 video notes and a semiconductor short-answer bank (`Recordings/`). |
| `Admin/` | TA announcements from the class Discord and the Canvas announcements, as text. |

Not included: videos, images, PDFs, the textbook (Ulaby, Maharbiz and Furse, *Circuit Analysis and Design* 3e, open access), the Fall 2025 Exam 2, personal study plans and logs, submitted work.

Numbers in the notes were re-derived in Python. Disagreements with a source are flagged in `[!warning]` callouts and unreadable items as `(?)`. Transcripts are machine output.

**Keep this repository private.** The syllabus restricts class recordings to students in the class, and the lecture transcripts come from those recordings. Past exams and official solutions are the instructor's material, and the syllabus forbids using problem solutions obtained from other students.
